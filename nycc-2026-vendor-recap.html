Reach, per booth

  • 3,177 ConLoot accounts by Sunday night, up 83% in the 19 days around the show. 1,657 of them signed up during the NYCC run.
  • Verified vendors drew 3× the views of the median listed vendor: 263 views per verified vendor vs. 89 across all 240 vendors with NYCC items. The top vendor page cleared 1,800 views.
  • 541 new vendor follows during the window. Total follows nearly tripled, 547 → 1,587. A follow means a push notification the next time you add an item or a show.
  • 61% of catalogued items (875 of 1,441) were saved by at least one collector; 47 items had 10 or more saves.
  • Featured placement: 4 spotlight vendors, 65,400 impressions, 444 taps across the window.

When collectors were looking

  • Planning starts about two weeks out and compounds: daily saves went 35 → 146 → 316 → 406 across the final week before doors.
  • The single busiest hour for want-list saves was 11 pm ET the night before doors (Oct 7). Booth schedules peaked at 8 am ET on opening day.
  • Thursday carried the floor: 6,656 item views and 1,899 booth views on Oct 8, then 3,861 Friday, 2,569 Saturday, 1,049 Sunday. Early-week listings get the most eyes.
