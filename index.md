# ConLoot

**Track convention exclusives, drops, and prices.**

ConLoot is a mobile app for collectors and flippers attending San Diego Comic-Con and other major conventions. Browse the exclusives lineup, save what you're hunting, plan your con schedule, get notified when new items drop, and check real eBay prices before you queue.

## Get the app

**[Download ConLoot on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6772773090)** — free on iOS.

Android is in closed testing and coming soon.

## What's inside

- The full exclusives catalog — browse by vendor, booth, category, or price
- Offline-first lists: save your hunt list and check items off on the floor, no signal needed
- A schedule planner for drops, signings, and booth runs
- eBay price comps with a confidence rating on every item
- Push alerts for new items and vendor updates
