# ConLoot

**Track convention exclusives, drops, and prices.**

ConLoot is a mobile app for collectors and flippers attending San Diego Comic-Con and other major conventions. Browse the exclusives lineup, save what you're hunting, get notified the moment drops go live, and check eBay comps before you queue.

## Get the app

The iOS App Store and Google Play listings go live before SDCC 2026. Until then, check back here.
