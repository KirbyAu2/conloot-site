# Delete your ConLoot™ account and data

You can delete your ConLoot account and all associated data at any time. There are
two ways to do it.

## Option 1 — Delete in the app (immediate)

1. Open the ConLoot app.
2. Go to the **Account** tab.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete Account**.
4. Confirm.

This permanently removes your profile, saved lists, list items, vendor follows, and
push notification tokens. The action cannot be undone.

## Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email **[conlootapp@gmail.com](mailto:conlootapp@gmail.com)**
from the address associated with your account and ask us to delete it. We will verify
your request and complete the deletion on your behalf.

## What gets deleted, and when

When you delete your account, we delete the following from our active databases within
**30 days**:

- Your account record (email, name, hashed password)
- Your saved lists and list items
- Your vendor follows and event subscriptions
- Your push notification tokens and notification preferences

Backups that contain your data are retained on a rolling **35-day** cycle and are
overwritten thereafter. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke your Apple
authorization token as part of deletion.

For full details, see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).
