ConLoot reaches out to vendors exhibiting at shows we're building coverage for. If a note arrived from one of the channels below, it came from Orlando — the person who builds the app.

Orlando's address. Most vendor outreach comes from here.

Email — general

Email — automated

notify@conloot.app

That's the whole list. No other handle, address, or platform is us. If something claims to be ConLoot and isn't on this page, it isn't — forward it to conlootapp@gmail.com and we'll tell you.

What we will never ask you for

  • Money. Listing on ConLoot is free. We will never ask you to pay for a listing, a badge, placement, or a "featured" slot.
  • Passwords. Not yours, not your shop's, not for any platform — including ours. We have no reason to know them.
  • Bank or payment details. No account numbers, no card details, no payment processor logins. We don't handle your money at all.
  • Personal information. A business contact email is all we need. No ID, no home address, no social security or tax numbers.

There's no upsell at the end of this. Getting listed is the whole ask — there is no call, no demo, no plan to pitch you afterwards. Here's how ConLoot actually pays for itself.
