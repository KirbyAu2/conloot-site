Straight answers

Is this a data grab?

No. No ads, no data sales, no cross-app tracking — of you or of collectors. Your listing data is public catalog information you enter, edit, and can take down. Read the privacy policy →

What's the catch?

Affiliate links. ConLoot earns a small eBay commission when someone buys through an outbound price-comp link. That's the whole business model, and it costs you and them nothing. How ConLoot makes money →

Who's behind this?

A person, not a growth team. Orlando Salvatore — a game-industry engineer who's been going to SDCC for twenty-odd years and built the tool he wanted. Meet the founder →

Did you message me?

Probably, yes. We reach out to vendors exhibiting at shows we're covering. Here are the only channels we ever contact you from, and what we'll never ask you for. Check it's really us →
