# Support

Need help with ConLoot™? We respond within one business day.

**Email:** [conlootapp@gmail.com](mailto:conlootapp@gmail.com)

## Common questions

### How do I delete my account?
Open the app → **Account** tab → scroll to the bottom → tap **Delete Account**. This permanently removes your profile, saved lists, and follows. The action cannot be undone.

### Saved lists aren't syncing across my devices
Lists are stored locally first and sync in the background after sign-in. Make sure you're signed into the same account on both devices and have a working internet connection on both. If you're signed in but the list still isn't appearing, force-quit and reopen the app to trigger a fresh sync.

### Push notifications aren't arriving
- Confirm notifications are enabled in iOS/Android Settings → ConLoot.
- Confirm the specific notification category (Vendor activity, Updates & New Releases, etc.) is on in the in-app **Account → Notifications** section.

### A booth or item is wrong
Email [conlootapp@gmail.com](mailto:conlootapp@gmail.com) with the convention, vendor name, and what's incorrect. We update the data as soon as we can verify it.

### How are eBay prices calculated?
We pull sold-listings data via the eBay API and compute a rolling median. The "confidence" chip on each price reflects how many comparable sales we found in the last 30 days. Low confidence means few or noisy data points — treat those prices as estimates.

### I got a message from someone at ConLoot — is it legit?
Almost certainly yes. We reach out to vendors exhibiting at shows we're building coverage for. [conloot.app/hello](/hello/) lists the only handles and addresses we ever contact people from, and what we will never ask you for (payment, passwords, bank details). If a message doesn't match that page, forward it to [conlootapp@gmail.com](mailto:conlootapp@gmail.com) and we'll confirm.

### Is ConLoot free? Will it stay free?
The app is free, and listing as a vendor is free. ConLoot earns a small commission when someone buys through an outbound eBay price-comp link — that's the whole business model, there are no ads, and it costs you nothing. If a paid tier ever exists it will be optional extras announced in advance, and what's free today stays free. The full explanation is on the [About page](/about/#money).

### Does ConLoot sell my data?
No. No ads, no data sales, no cross-app tracking. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).

### I want to claim my vendor booth
Tap a booth → **About** tab → **Claim this booth**. Fill in your vendor name and contact email. We'll verify and grant access within one business day.
